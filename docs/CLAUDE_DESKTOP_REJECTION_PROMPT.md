== 1. WHAT THE PRODUCT IS ==
Numbite is a free UK iPhone app (and website https://numbite.com) for people who count calories or protein. Three parts: Eat out (full nutrition for menu items at ~241 UK
restaurant chains, copied by script from each chain's own published guide; filters for diet and allergies; "find restaurants by your numbers"; Near me map), Shop (products
from nine UK supermarkets with their prices and photos, barcode scanner, compare tool, shopping list that can be shared by link) and Cook (117 recipes built from real
Sainsbury's products; every figure worked out from the product labels). Also: daily targets worked out from onboarding answers, a Home log of meals and water, reminders
(local notifications, e.g. for medication, no medical advice), short food articles, and optional accounts (Sign in with Apple / Google / email code) with sync across devices
and a Community part (friends by code or invite link, send a shopping list, report/block). The app never invents or estimates a nutrition number: a figure that is not
published says "not published". It is English only, light appearance only.

== 2. HOW IT IS BUILT ==
- Repo: MenuAi (GitHub aman66-web/menuai, it is also the folder on this Mac). The product is the WEB APP in web/ (Next.js 16, TypeScript) packed inside a thin SwiftUI/WKWebView
  iPhone app: scripts/build_app_web.sh builds a static export into MenuMacros/Resources/WebApp.bundle (git-ignored, ~160 MB), the app serves it on app://menumath/ and has
  native bridges for the barcode scanner, location, share sheet and local notifications. Xcode project MenuMacros.xcodeproj, scheme MenuMacros, bundle ID
  com.amanmarwaha.MenuMacros, team S7G6ZHHK59 (CLARIFO DEVELOPERS LTD), Xcode 26.6, iPhone 17 Pro simulator, iOS 17+.
- The live site/API: Vercel (project menumacros, Production branch main, domain numbite.com) + Supabase (project menumacros, ref rbbalkfkpacazzhsqfzz). The app reads newer
  menus, hidden.json and the API from numbite.com (CORS allows only the origin app://menumath). DO NOT break the live site or API.
- Menu data is generated by scripts from data/source/ into web/public/menus; never hand-edit it. Grocery lists are in web/public/groceries.
- What goes inside the iPhone app is controlled by web/app-content.json: appLogos (the chains' and supermarkets' own logo files; false = cuisine icons and plain names),
  menuPhotos (the chains' own item photos), shops (the nine supermarkets packed in). A logo/photo/shop can also be taken down WITHOUT an app update by listing it in
  web/public/logos/hidden.json and deploying the website.
