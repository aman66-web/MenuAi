**Subject:** Request: permission or a data feed to show Sainsbury's store stock status inside a free UK nutrition app

Hello,

I'm the founder of Numbite, a free UK iPhone app and website (https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/numbite-calories-protein/id6822767746) that helps people count calories and protein when they eat out, shop and cook. Our recipes are built from Sainsbury's products, using the names, pack sizes and shelf prices on sainsburys.co.uk.

People often ask whether the ingredients in a recipe are in stock at their local store. Your Store Stock Checker (stockchecker.sainsburys.co.uk) answers exactly that, and we would much rather use it with your agreement than send people to retype every ingredient.

Could you tell me whether Sainsbury's offers, or would consider offering:

1. an official way (an API, a data feed or written permission) for Numbite to read the stock status the Stock Checker shows for a product at a store the person chooses; or
2. permission to open the Stock Checker from our app with the store and product already filled in (a link we build ourselves), if that is the only route you would support.

How we would use it, if you agree:
- We would show your four labels exactly as you give them ("In Stock", "Awaiting Delivery", "Not Available", "Not Tracked") with your own note that availability is an indication, and say that the information comes from Sainsbury's.
- Only when the person taps "Check stock", and only for the products in the recipe or shopping list they are looking at.
- We would not store a person's postcode or chosen store on our servers, and we would not keep or resell stock data.
- We would follow any limits, terms and branding rules you set, and we would not suggest that Sainsbury's is affiliated with or endorses Numbite.

Until we hear from you we will not read the Stock Checker automatically; the app will only link people to your own page.

Thank you for your time. I can send screenshots of how it would look.

Kind regards,
[Your full name]
Founder, Numbite
[Your email address and phone number]
