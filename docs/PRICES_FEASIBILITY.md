# Prices from the nearest branch: what is possible, chain by chain

Findings of a read-only feasibility check on 2026-10-06 (nothing was scraped into the repo, nothing is published). Prices are NOT in the
app yet. Rule that would apply (CLAUDE.md rule 4): the phone picks the nearest branch itself; only a branch id may ever be sent anywhere.

Counts by verdict: feasible-per-branch: 29, blocked: 24, no-prices-published: 15, feasible-national-or-band-only: 4

## What it would take

- A new data layer: each chain gets price *bands* (many chains price by region/band, e.g. Zizzi Aberdeen vs Chiswick differ by 30p on a drink), each branch maps to a band, each item has one price per band.
- Per-chain adapters (the platforms group: Azzurri = Zizzi + ASK + Coco di Mama share one; Big Table Group = Bella Italia, Frankie & Benny's, Las Iguanas, Banana Tree, Cafe Rouge, Chiquito share one).
- Prices change often (weekly to monthly): a refresh routine and a "price checked on {date}, may differ in your branch" label, never presented as exact.
- Terms: Wagamama's terms forbid text/data mining and web scraping; PizzaExpress, Five Guys, Prezzo, Pret and Greggs restrict commercial use of site content. Needs the founder's call per chain, like photos.
- Recommended first step if you want it: the Azzurri group and the Big Table Group (9 chains, one adapter each, prices are server-rendered or in published menu pages, robots.txt allows them).
