Each figure is for one dish in the portion size shown (Value, Regular or Large) from a lab analysis dated Jan 2025; stores may vary slightly. A box is two bases, a saucy and a dry dish, so add them up. Wraps, bao, bubble tea and desserts have no figures on the page. Torpedo Prawns and Oodles Crispy Chicken are left out: their calories contradict their own kJ.
