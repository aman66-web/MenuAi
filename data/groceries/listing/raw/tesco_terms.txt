# Tesco terms check (read 2026-10-09 in my own Chrome tab, UK VPN on)

## robots.txt (https://www.tesco.com/robots.txt), User-agent: *  (no Crawl-delay; no blanket Disallow)
Rules that touch category/listing URLs (everything else is Allow by default):
  Disallow: /shop/*-*/*&offset=*        (listing URLs carrying "&offset=")
  Disallow: *include-children*
  Disallow: *currentModal*
  Disallow: *active-tab*
  Disallow: *new&new*
  Disallow: /shop/*-*/promotions/*?*=*  (promotion pages with query parameters)
  Disallow: /shop/*-*/products*?icid, /shop/*-*/zone*?icid, /shop/*-*/buylists*?icid, /shop/*-*/slots*?icid
  Disallow: *?queueittoken*   Disallow: */userinteraction*   Disallow: */nrbeacon/*   (plus login/register/password URLs)
The category pages I need (https://www.tesco.com/groceries/en-GB/shop/... and https://www.tesco.com/shop/en-GB/browse/.../all, plain or with ?page=N) match none of these.
I will NOT use any URL containing &offset=, include-children, currentModal, active-tab, new&new, or promotions pages.
Sitemaps listed: https://www.tesco.com/sitemaps/en-GB/groceries/products-index.xml (and others).
