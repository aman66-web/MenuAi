Lines look like `abcd|g|426|103|7.6|5.1|4.2|4.2|<0.5|4.0|0.10|ef12`. The basis (second field) is `g` or `ml` for a plain per-100 column, or `g:grilled`, `g:cooked`, `ml:prepared`... when the table's heading adds a word (the app labels those numbers with the word), or `?`. Copy every line exactly. Empty fields are normal (the table lacks that row). `NONE|abcd` means the page has no nutrition table.
