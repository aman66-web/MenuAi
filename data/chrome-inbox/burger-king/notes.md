# Burger King UK: notes (read 2026-10-10)

Source: each product's page on https://www.burgerking.co.uk/menu/... (robots.txt: only /bcc and /rewards/details are disallowed). Every product page has a 'Nutritional Information & Allergens' button; the panel prints, per serving, energy (kcal and kJ), carbohydrates, sugars, fat, saturates, protein and salt, plus Contains / May contain / May contain via shared equipment allergens. Meal pages list their components too (burger, fries, drink), which is how side items were read. Cookies were rejected.
- Allergens: 'may_contain' here is the union of the panel's 'May contain' and 'May contain via shared equipment' lines (the safe direction). 'Sulphur dioxide' is written 'sulphites'. Big King Double Royale and Big King Royale print no allergen block, so theirs are blank (not NONE).
- Copied as printed, but inconsistent on the chain's own page: WHOPPER with Bacon and Cheese (721 kcal; macros imply about 797) and 6pc Onion Rings (225 kcal; macros imply about 274). Sugars read 0.7 g on many items (fries, nuggets, cheesecake bars...) and look like a placeholder. Gooey Salted Caramel Cheesecake Bar prints 285 kcal while its macros imply about 234. Bacon and Egg Sandwich prints exactly the same energy and macros as Ultimate Breakfast sandwich (salt differs) and Sausage and Egg sandwich prints 6 g protein: copied as printed.
- Not read (BK has about 130 products on the menu): about 20 more sandwiches/meals I did not open, most drinks (a drink panel prints only energy and no size, so it was not written), dips, sauces, King Jr toys, the Frozen Fanta range, Ben & Jerry's, Fusion and sundaes. Component rows (Kids Hamburger, Small Fries, 9 Chilli Cheese Bites...) came from meal/bundle pages.
- Photos (2026-10-10): 38 of 53 rows have a photo in `photos/` (listed in `photos.csv`; the source addresses are in `images.csv`). Each address is Burger King's own picture for that product's page on cdn.sanity.io (the original file, no quality or size parameters), read from the page's network requests after opening the product's own page, and used only where the page's heading names the same product (three have a trivial spelling difference: 'Plant Based WHOPPER' for Plant-based WHOPPER, '9 pc' for 9pc, '6 Chilli Cheese Bites*' with a footnote star). No photo for 15 rows: Large Fries, Small Fries, Regular Fries, 3/4/9 Chilli Cheese Bites, 4pc Kansas BBQ Chicken Fries, Kids Hamburger, Classic Double Beef Melt, Bacon BBQ Chicken Stacker and Bacon BBQ Stacker Melt Burger (their page_url is a different product's page, e.g. a bundle or 'Kansas BBQ King Box'), BBQ Steakhouse Angus (page says 'Steakhouse Angus Gourmet'), Chicken Royale Bacon & Cheese (page says 'Cheese & Bacon'), Kids Nuggets (page says 'Kids Chicken Nuggets Meal Small'), and Kids Cheeseburger (its picture is the whole kids' box meal, the same rule as Wendy's). cdn.sanity.io's robots.txt allowed the fetch (RFC 9309 matcher in images_common). Checked on a contact sheet on white: every picture shows its product.
- Sitemap used to find product pages: https://www.burgerking.co.uk/sitemap.xml (it lists 40 picker and 42 item pages; the menu page has more).
- Added later on 2026-10-10: Bacon Butty (item_42932; panel read in full, kcal agrees with its macros). No photo for it yet (its page was already cached in the browser, so the picture's request was not logged). Pausing there: further item pages (Hash Browns item_45090, a Ben & Jerry's dessert item_57804 and about 15 more not yet named) kept showing the site's own 'lost network connectivity' dialog and loaded slowly after many page loads in a row, so I stopped rather than keep requesting. Standalone Hamburger, Cheeseburger and Double Cheeseburger pages (item_1851, item_1854, item_1870) exist but those three burgers already have rows from their meal pages. Drinks (coffees, tea, milk, water), ketchup and dip pots were not read (a drink panel prints no size).
