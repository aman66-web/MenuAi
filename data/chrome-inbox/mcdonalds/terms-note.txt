McDonald's UK Terms & Conditions, https://www.mcdonalds.com/gb/en-gb/terms-and-conditions.html (read 2026-10-09), section 8 "Acceptable uses and restrictions for the online services":
  "d) Use, copy, distribute, republish, display, disclose, upload, post, or transmit the online services in any commercial manner;"
  "h) Use or launch any unauthorised technology or automated system to access the online services or extract content from the online services, including but not limited to spiders, robots, screen scrapers, or offline readers;"
The "online services" definition includes content. robots.txt (https://www.mcdonalds.com/robots.txt) disallows only /config/, /utility/, /content/prelaunch/, /prelaunch/ and one US page; /gb/ product pages and /content/dam/ are allowed.
