# Turtle Bay: notes for the importer (read 2026-10-10)

Source: the Bath restaurant menu at https://turtlebay.co.uk/menu/bath. Each dish's 'More info' pop-up was opened one at a time.
Menus differ per restaurant (43 restaurants in the dropdown); only Bath was read.
Calories only: the pop-up shows one kcal figure per dish (no protein, carbs or fat). Drinks and alcohol were not read.
The pop-up is headed 'Allergenic ingredients in this dish' (with kcal under it), or 'Caloric information for this dish' with no allergen list; in that case the contains column is left blank (not NONE).
