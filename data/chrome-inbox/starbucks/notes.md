# Starbucks UK: notes (read 2026-10-10)

Source: each food product's page on https://www.starbucks.co.uk/menu/product/<id> (robots.txt disallows /api/ and any .pdf, not these pages; the nutrition page https://www.starbucks.co.uk/nutrition says drinks show no allergen information and points to PDF booklets). Each page prints per serving: energy (kJ and kcal), fat, saturates, carbohydrates, sugar, fibre, protein and salt, and the allergens in capitals ('EGG, MILK, and WHEAT'; 'Spelt' and 'Rye' are named cereals).
- 56 food items: 10 sandwiches/wraps, 12 breakfast and pastries, 16 bakery, 18 snacks and treats, all as listed on the category pages on 10 October 2026 (seasonal items such as the Pumpkin range will change).
- Banana Nut Loaf Cake prints fat as '368.0g' (the kcal figure; impossible): copied as printed, the cloud audit should hold it back. Mini Waffles is a sharing bag (787 kcal for the bag). Kettle Chips Lightly Salted and Sweet & Salty Popcorn print no allergen line, so their allergens are blank (not NONE).
- Not read: drinks (hot, iced, Frappuccino, matcha, bottled): they print energy and macros for the default size and milk only and no allergens; Starbucks at Home coffee and merchandise.
- Photos (2026-10-10): all 56 rows have a photo in `photos/` (listed in `photos.csv`, source addresses in `images.csv`). Each address is the picture shown at the top of that item's own page (starbucks.co.uk/menu/product/<id>), read from the page's network requests after opening the page, and fetched from Starbucks' own media host www.digitalassets.starbucks.eu (the original 1500 px file, resized to 640 px WebP only; robots.txt on that host allowed it under the RFC 9309 check in images_common). Three downloads failed once on a network error (a timeout, a DNS lookup, a reset: not refusals) and succeeded on the second run. Checked on a contact sheet: every picture shows its product (packaged items show the pack, e.g. the Kettle Chips bags).
