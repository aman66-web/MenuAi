Source: each food product's page on https://www.starbucks.co.uk/menu/product/<id> (robots.txt disallows /api/ and any .pdf, not these pages; the nutrition page https://www.starbucks.co.uk/nutrition says drinks show no allergen information and points to PDF booklets).
